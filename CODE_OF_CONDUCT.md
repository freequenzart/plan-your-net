# Community Code of Conduct

Plan Your Net welcomes contributors and users with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected conduct

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient. Discuss the work rather than attacking the person.
- Welcome questions and acknowledge accessibility and language differences.
- Respect privacy and consent. Share only information that you have permission to publish.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized behavior, and disclosure of others' personal information.
- Keep project spaces useful: no spam, impersonation, or sustained disruption.

## Moderation

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, request changes, or restrict participation. Responses should reflect the seriousness and repetition of the behavior. Where practical, explain decisions without disclosing private reports or personal information.

## Reporting concerns

For moderation concerns, contact the repository maintainer [@freequenzart](https://github.com/freequenzart) privately through GitHub. Do not post incident details, names of affected people, or sensitive screenshots publicly. If GitHub does not provide a private contact route, request one without including the details. If a concern is also a software vulnerability, follow SECURITY.md.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed community spaces. It does not promise constant moderation availability.
